# Exclusions and limits

- The complete text and evidentiary annexes of AMCU decision `535-р` were not
  located in the official public sources inventoried on 2026-08-21. This case
  reconstructs the published administrative outcome, not the authority's full
  reasoning.
- The committed Prozorro fixture is a data-minimized projection. The acquisition
  receipt records SHA-256 digests of the complete API responses, but the complete
  responses and bid documents are not republished here.
- Public participation, price, and document metadata do not independently prove
  coordination, intent, guilt, or legal liability.
- The PDF metadata audit is an exploratory null result over 59 public PDFs. It
  is not an exhaustive forensic examination of archives, signatures, deleted
  revisions, network logs, banking data, or evidence held by AMCU.
- Company names and identifiers are retained because they are necessary to bind
  the official decision to the public tender records. Unnecessary personal and
  contact data were excluded from the committed projection.
- Appeals, judicial review, enforcement, payment of fines, and later changes to
  the official records are outside the frozen acquisition snapshot.
